How to get your _Bearer cookie? (click to expand)
  1. Open https://www.powertokens.ai in Chrome/Edge and log in.
  2. Press F12 → switch to the Application tab.
  3. Left side: expand Cookies → https://www.powertokens.ai.
  4. Find the row named _Bearer, double-click to copy its Value.
  5. Paste it into the box above, then click Load dashboard.

Your token is stored only in this browser's localStorage and is sent only to powertokens.ai — no third-party server ever sees it.
